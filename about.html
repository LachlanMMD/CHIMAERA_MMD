Chimaera Collective is a Copenhagen-based creative collective exploring the intersection of art, culture and community.

FROM GREEK MYTHOLOGY

THE NAME CHIMAERA

CHIMERA CPH takes its name from the mythological chimera, a creature made up of different beings combined into one. Traditionally seen as a fusion of multiple animals, the chimera represents transformation, contradiction, imagination, and the creation of something entirely new through collaboration.

EVT ANDEN KNAP →

In the same spirit, CHIMERA CPH is a collective space where different art forms, people, and creative processes come together. Visual artists, musicians, performers, designers, and audiences merge into a shared experience where art is not only observed, but participated in.

Like the chimera itself, the project is built from many parts, different disciplines, backgrounds, and ideas, forming something dynamic, open, and alive.
