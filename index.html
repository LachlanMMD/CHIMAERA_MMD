Manifesto

A SPACE FOR MAKING TOGETHER

CHIMAERA creates temporary spaces for collective experimentation. We treat art less as performance and more as participation. Less as product and more as process. Through food, sound, installation, and communal practice, we build environments where people can make, test, and share together.
